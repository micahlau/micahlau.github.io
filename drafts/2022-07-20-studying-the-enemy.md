title: Studying the Enemy
---

Lately, I have taken up a literary exercise I call "studying the enemy." I set out to read authors I have heard denounced as problematic, narcissistic, irritating, or otherwise have been warned against. My goal is to find out what exactly made anyone sit up and notice them in the first place. 

I read the stories of John Updike ("a penis with a thesaurus" -- David Foster Wallace), David Foster Wallace's self-indulgent essays, Jonathan Franzen's *The Corrections* ("I simply can't stand him" -- a high school mentor), stuff by the Brat Pack and the Beats and Ayn Rand. 

Then I had the idea to read snarky two-sentence reviews and snarky overlong reviews on Goodreads. Click on the authors of those reviews, read all their other reviews. I read the books they call "the front-runner for the worst thing I read this year," or "Recommended for: No One Ever," my expectations low. 

I don't have a unified take-away from my experience of studying these enemies. Their writings all have moments of beauty, massive blunders, and downright hideousness. Give anything a chance.
